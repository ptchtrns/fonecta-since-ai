Suggestions reach the owner one at a time, the most important first, and the next one only after they've answered. Settings are on the Connections page.

## Channels

WhatsApp, Telegram (connect the bot with one tap and answer with its buttons) and email through Resend (with a link to a page that needs no login). Messages go to the preferred channel from the customer record plus every channel turned on here. Text messages and app notifications, and a preferred channel that isn't connected yet, are recorded in an outbox shown on the Connections page.

## Language

Messages are in Finnish, Swedish, Norwegian or English, the same on every channel.

## Which suggestions are sent

"Send suggestions from" sets the least important one that goes out: low (all), medium or high. Lower ones wait on the dashboard. The closed-for-good question and suggestions a linked page disagrees with are never sent.

## Contact hours

Messages wait for the owner's contact hours (for example 12–16), in the business's time zone. The check runs every hour.

## Opting out

An owner who opts out gets no suggestion messages on any channel; suggestions still show on the dashboard. Turning messages back on sends the next suggestion within contact hours.

## Addresses

WhatsApp numbers, email addresses and the Telegram connection are set on the Connections page itself.
