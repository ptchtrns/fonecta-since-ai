Changes go to Yext, which sends them on to Google, Facebook, Bing, Apple Maps, directories and the other sites where the listing is live.

## When publishing is connected

An approved change is written to Yext straight away. For hours, Connecta AI reads the hours Yext has now and changes only what was approved, because Yext replaces the whole hours object. The confirmation says which sites show it and how soon: for example "Published to 11 sites. Google shows it within …, the slowest within …", with each site's typical update time.

## When it isn't

The change is saved in Connecta AI and shown on the dashboard, and the confirmation says it goes live once publishing to Yext is connected.

## If Yext refuses

Nothing is recorded, so a suggestion stays open and a business card edit isn't saved. The message shows Yext's reason. In the demo, the dataset's Yext mock accepts only hours and the business name, so other fields show its error.

## Live sites and update speed

Visibility → Listings shows each site, whether the listing is live there, and how long a change typically takes to show. "Not connected" on Google or Facebook means the owner's own account isn't linked: the listing can still be live, but the owner can't reply to reviews there.

## Businesses from the Yext account

They're read from Yext each time the dashboard opens, so changes made in Yext directly show up too. Their sites come from Yext's Listings API.
