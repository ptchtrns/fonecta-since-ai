The Reports page and the front page's numbers show how people find the business.

## Google statistics

Views on Google Search and Maps, searches, actions such as directions or website clicks, and calls, for the latest period with the change against the period before. For a business from the Yext account they come from Yext Analytics, which may not have data for every account; then the page says why.

## Expected lookups

Each suggestion says how many people are expected to look the business up on that day, from the Google views on the same day last season, or an average day's views when there's no such day.

## Suggestions so far

How many were suggested, answered, published, saved but not live yet, declined and applied automatically, and on which channel the owner answered.
