Google users and other sites can suggest changes to a listing, such as different hours or that the business has closed. They wait for the owner on the front page.

## Accepting

For a business from the Yext account, the answer goes to Yext, which applies an accepted change and sends it to every site. For the dataset's businesses, an accepted change to the hours is written like any other change (published when publishing is connected) and the answer is kept here.

## Rejecting

The listing stays as it is.

## "Closed" suggestions

Accepting one never takes the listing down by itself: a person at Fonecta checks first.
