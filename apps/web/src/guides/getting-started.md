Connecta AI (Fonecta Connect) keeps a business's listing right on Google, Apple Maps, Bing, Facebook and other sites, and so in AI assistants that answer from listing data. An account manages one business, chosen when signing up.

## Front page

The question box, the four headline numbers (profile strength, local search, AI visibility, Google views), the next suggestion for a special day (all of them on request), edits suggested by Google users, connections, profile, listings and reviews. The sidebar shows how many things are waiting on each page.

## Company information

The business card and its edit form: name, phone, website, street address, postal code, description and weekly opening hours, plus pages linked as sources. Also the profile checklist and public holidays without hours.

## Visibility

Three tabs. Listings: the sites the business shows on, whether each is live, how fast each picks up a change, and accounts to link. SEO: the local search score and what's behind it. AI: what assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini or Siri can answer about the business from its listing.

## Reports

Profile strength, Google statistics, what happened to the suggestions, what Connecta AI found, how the owner answers, and reviews.

## Connections

How suggestions reach the owner: WhatsApp, Telegram and email, the message language, which suggestions are sent (by importance), standing permission for public holidays, and contact preferences (preferred channel, opting out).

## Account

Sign-in details, and which business the account manages.
