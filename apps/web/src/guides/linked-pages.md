Under Company information the owner can link up to 10 public pages that show the business's hours, such as its website or Facebook page.

## What they're for

Each suggestion is compared with the hours found on those pages. When a page agrees, the suggestion says so. When it disagrees, the suggestion shows a warning, isn't sent in messages and isn't applied under standing permission.

## How often they're read

Once a day, early in the morning (05:00 UTC), and when a page is added. Some sites block automatic reading; those show as unreadable.

## Changing them

Links are added and removed on Company information. The copilot can't change them.
