The copilot answers from this business's own data in the dashboard and from these guides. It has no web access, can't send messages or emails, and can't contact anyone.

## Changes need your confirmation

When you ask for a change, the copilot prepares a preview: each field as it is now and as it would be, and what else happens (for example "Published to Yext as soon as you confirm"). Nothing is saved until you press Confirm. Cancel discards it. A preview expires after 15 minutes, and if something changes in the meantime (you edit the same field on the page, or a suggestion is answered elsewhere) confirming refuses, and you can ask for a fresh preview.

## What it can change

Business details (name, phone, website, street address, postal code, description), weekly opening hours, answers to special-day suggestions (approve, edit, decline), answers to edits suggested by Google users, message settings (language, which suggestions are sent, standing permission), and contact preferences (opting out, preferred channel).

## What it can't change

Pages linked as sources, WhatsApp, Telegram and email addresses, hours for a single date that has no suggestion, the "closed for good" question, sign-in details, and which business the account manages. Those are done on their pages; the copilot tells you where.

## Your conversations

Conversations are kept for 30 days after the last message, then deleted. You can delete one yourself from the copilot's menu. A log of what was changed and when (without the text of your messages) is kept for a year.
