A business has weekly opening hours and special hours for single dates.

## Weekly hours

Set per weekday on Company information, or with the copilot. Each day is either closed or one or more time ranges, such as 10:00–14:00 and 17:00–22:00 for a lunch break. The copilot writes them as "closed" or "10:00-14:00,17:00-22:00". Weekly hours apply every week; dates with their own special hours keep them.

## Special hours for a date

Hours for one date (a holiday, an eve, a school break) come from answering a suggestion on the front page: approve, or edit to your own hours. There's no separate form for a date without a suggestion; Connecta AI suggests the dates that matter, most important first.

## Holidays without hours

Company information and the profile checklist list public holidays in the next 60 days that fall on a normally open day and have no hours. Google may show the business as open on them.

## Open now

Map sites and assistants work out "open now" from the weekly hours with special hours on top, in the business's time zone.
