Three scores, each out of 100, are worked out from the business's details, its listings, reviews and suggested edits. Each is a list of checks; a check that's done adds its points. The points are rules of thumb on what Google and assistants weigh.

## Profile strength

How much of what customers look for the profile has: opening hours 25, phone number 15, description of at least 10 characters 15, holiday hours for public holidays in the next 60 days 15, website 10, street address 10, live on Google 10.

## Local search (SEO tab)

What Google weighs for map results and what makes people click: business category 10, a description of at least 250 characters 15 (Google shows up to 750), the description names the city 10, opening hours 15, holiday hours 10, website 10, phone 10, reviews 15, and Google 5 (live, and the owner's own Google account linked where that's known). Reviews count when there are as many as the typical similar business in the country has (the median across them) at 4 stars or better on average; without enough similar businesses, 10 reviews.

## AI visibility (AI tab)

What assistants need to answer about the business: opening hours 20, holiday hours 15, a description of at least 80 characters so an assistant can say what you offer 20, both phone and website 10, listed on Google (Gemini and Google AI Overviews) 8, on Bing (ChatGPT search and Microsoft Copilot) 8, on Apple Maps (Siri) 8, and at least 5 reviews 11.

## How to raise them

The checks marked as fixed on Company information (hours, phone, website, address, description) are changed on the business card, or with the copilot. Holiday hours are fixed by answering the suggestions on the front page. Being live on a site and linking the owner's own accounts are on Visibility → Listings. Reviews come from customers.

## Holiday hours check

It's done when every public holiday in the next 60 days that falls on a day the business is normally open has its own hours. Otherwise Google may show the business as open when it isn't.
