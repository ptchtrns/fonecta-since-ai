"Handle public holidays without asking" lets Connecta AI apply public-holiday suggestions itself.

## What it applies

Suggestions for public holidays and their eves where the predictor is at least 70% sure and no linked page disagrees. Everything else still waits for the owner.

## When

Straight away when it's turned on, then within the owner's contact hours on the hourly run.

## Being told

The owner gets one message listing what was updated, for example "As agreed, we've updated the hours for Tilitoimisto Otso: Fri 25.12. closed, Fri 1.1. closed." Reports count these as answered automatically.

## Turning it off

Turn it off on the Connections page, or ask the copilot. Suggestions then wait for the owner's answer again.
