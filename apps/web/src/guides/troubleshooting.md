Common messages and what to do about them.

## "Google and the other sites didn't accept it"

Yext refused the change, and nothing was saved. The message includes Yext's reason. In the demo, the dataset's Yext mock accepts only hours and the business name.

## "It goes live once publishing to Yext is connected"

The change is saved in Connecta AI but no Yext connection is configured for this business, so the sites don't have it yet.

## "Something changed since this preview was made"

The copilot's preview was based on values that have changed since (an edit on the page, an answer on another channel, or a new prediction). Nothing was saved. Ask again for a fresh preview.

## "This preview expired"

Previews are valid for 15 minutes. Ask again.

## "No language model is connected"

The copilot and typed questions need a model. The front page's ready-made questions still answer from the data.

## A suggestion disappeared

It was answered on another channel (WhatsApp, Telegram, the email link), applied under standing permission, or the predictor no longer suggests it after a newer answer.

## Messages don't arrive

Check that the owner hasn't opted out, the channel is connected on the Connections page, and it's within contact hours. Suggestions below the chosen importance, and ones a linked page disagrees with, wait on the dashboard.
