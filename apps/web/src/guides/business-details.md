The business card on Company information holds what customers see: name, phone, website, street address, postal code, description and weekly hours. The city and country come from the listing and aren't edited here.

## Phone

With the country code, for example +358 40 123 4567. Without a phone number, Google doesn't show the Call button.

## Website

A link to the business's own site, menu or booking page. It gets more clicks from Google.

## Description

Up to 600 characters here. Say what you offer and where: at least 80 characters lets assistants describe the business, and at least 250 counts fully for local search. Name the city, because people search for "café Tampere". Write it for customers, not as a list of keywords.

## Name

The name as customers know it. Adding keywords or locations to the name goes against Google's guidelines.

## Saving

Saving publishes the changed fields to Yext when publishing is connected; otherwise they're saved here and go live once it is.
